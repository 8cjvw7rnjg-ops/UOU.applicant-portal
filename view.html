Before You Start

Please make sure you are using the correct application route.

Apply through UCAS if you are:

  • An applicant from UK or Ireland
  • Applying for full-time undergraduate degree programmes at our Belfast, Coleraine or Derry/Londonderry campuses.

To apply via UCAS, visit: Choose your future | UCAS

Apply through our International Applicant Portal if you are:

  • An international applicant
  • Applying for an undergraduate or postgraduate degree programme, including distance learning programmes, at one of our Northern Ireland campuses.

To apply via our International Applicant Portal, visit: International Applicant Portal
